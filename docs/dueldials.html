How-to guide

Duel Dials

A round, spinning life tracker for card games. One dial per player, all sharing the same game, with a companion app for your phone. This guide covers every feature, from the first power-on to rolling a natural 20.

01Controls at a glance

The dial has three ways to talk to it: turn the outer ring, press the screen in (the whole screen is a button), and touch the glass. Most screens use all three, so pick whichever feels natural.

Turn the dialChange the number in front of you, or move the highlight.
Press the screenChoose, confirm, or move to the next player on the table.
Press twice quicklyUndo the last change (on the table and the victory screen).
Press 3 times quicklyGo home to the game selector. Your game is kept under Resume.
Press and holdBack, or open the player's damage screen from the table.
Press in for 3 secondsReset: a red ring counts down, then the game selector opens for a new game.
Tap the glassPick a seat, a tile, a button or a number.
Touch, hold, let goOpen the game menu from the table.
Hold on a nameOpen that player's colour picker and name editor.
Drag seat to seatOpen an attack from one player to another.
The LED ring under the dial follows along: it glows in the focused player's colour, sweeps the five mana colours on the selector, pulses on a knockout, chases gold for a winner and spins while dice roll.

02Starting a game

At power-on the Duel Dials logo shows for two seconds, then the game selector. The same selector opens after a reset or a triple press.

  1. Pick a game. Tap Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana, Pokémon or Riftbound (or turn the dial and press). Resume carries on with the saved game, and DND Dice opens the dice roller.
  2. Magic only: pick the format. Commander (40 life), Standard (20), Archenemy (player 1 is the Archenemy with 20 extra life) or Emperor (two teams of three).
  3. Pick the number of players and tap Next. Each game offers its own range; Pokémon and Emperor skip this step.
  4. Choose names. Standard uses Player 1, Player 2 and so on. Custom walks through each player's name in turn (see Colours and names), then starts.
  5. The game starts on every linked dial. The logo plays, then an arrow spins round the table and lands on the player who goes first. Tap to skip it. In Archenemy the Archenemy always goes first.

To go back a step, tap the title or the arrow at the bottom, or press and hold the dial.

03The table

Each player gets a coloured seat around the edge, with their total turned to face them. The middle shows the focused player in large type.

  • Change life: turn the dial. A green or red +3 / −3 shows the running change.
  • Next player: press the screen, or tap a seat to jump to it.
  • Undo: press twice quickly. Quick bursts of changes undo together.
  • Status line: under the number you'll see the focused player's poison, commander damage and counters, then the turn and how many dials are linked.
  • Seat badges: a crown for the Monarch, the Initiative marker, the Archenemy's horns, emperors' laurels, and a small dial icon on seats where a dial sits. A knocked-out player's seat greys out with a skull.
  • Day and Night: when set from the menu, a big faint sun or moon sits behind the number.

04Damage and counters

Tap the middle of the table (or press and hold the dial) to open the focused player's damage screen: a grid of round tiles for everything you can track.

  • Tiles: life, commander damage from each opponent (tinted in their colour), poison, energy, experience, radiation, speed and tickets, then Monarch, Initiative, Name, Colour, Concede, Menu and Back. Swipe left or right for the second page.
  • Edit a value: tap a tile. The editor shows the damage type's icon large behind the number. Turn the dial, or tap − / + (hold them to repeat faster). Tap Done or press when finished.
  • Lethal rings: poison and commander damage show a ring filling toward lethal (10 poison, 21 from one commander) and turn red when reached.
  • Commander damage also lowers life by default. Turn this off in the menu with Cmdr hits life.
  • Partners: turn on Partners in the menu to track two commanders per opponent (shown as A and B).
  • Monarch and Initiative tiles toggle with one tap. Concede knocks the player out; tap again to rejoin.

05Drag to attack

Drag your finger from the attacking player's seat to the defending player's seat. An attack screen opens with the two names at the top.

  1. Set the amount with the dial or the − / + buttons. The line underneath previews the result, for example Life 45 > 42.
  2. Pick the type along the bottom: combat damage (sword), commander damage (shield, A or B with partners) or poison/infect (skull). The heart toggles lifelink so the attacker gains the same amount.
  3. Press the dial or tap the number to apply. The whole attack is one undo step. Hold the dial or tap the names to cancel.

In Yu-Gi-Oh! attacks start at 1000 and move in steps of 100. Lorcana, Pokémon and Riftbound don't use attacks, because nobody loses life.

06Colours and names

Touch and hold a player's name (in the middle of the table, or the title of their damage screen) to open their colour picker.

  • Colour: turn the dial or tap a swatch to browse; the LED ring previews each one. Press or tap the centre to choose; hold the dial to cancel. A dot marks colours another player already has.
  • Name: tap the name in the colour picker. Turn the dial to pick each letter, press to move to the next slot, or tap a slot to jump to it. Del removes a letter. Done or holding the dial saves.
  • Names are up to 8 characters. Leave one empty to go back to "Player N". Names and colours carry into rematches and show on every linked dial.

07Knockouts and winning

Players are knocked out automatically: life at 0 or below, 10 poison, 21 commander damage from one commander, or conceding. Every linked dial plays a short animation for how they went out.

  • Tap or press to skip a knockout animation. Undoing the change that caused it cancels it.
  • When the game is decided, confetti bursts in the winner's colour with a gold crown while the LEDs chase and flash gold. Teams and heroes win together.
  • Play again? appears after a moment. Press the dial or tap it to start a rematch with the same game, players, names and colours on every dial.
  • Press twice quickly to undo the winning hit instead. Hold the dial or tap elsewhere to look at the final board; press the dial there to bring the prompt back.

09DND Dice

Tap DND Dice on the game selector for a d4, d6, d8, d10, d20 and d100.

  • Pick a die from the ring around the edge (tap it, or turn the dial).
  • Tap the die or press the dial to roll. It tumbles while the numbers flicker and slow down, the LEDs spin in the die's colour, and the result lands with a pop.
  • On a d20, a 20 shows Natural 20! in gold and a 1 shows Critical fail.
  • The last four rolls run across the top. Tap the arrow or hold the dial to go back.

Dice stay on the dial you roll them on and never change the game.

11Linking dials

Dials talk to each other directly by radio, with no router needed. Dials in the same room share one game: every change, new game, spinner, knockout and undo shows on all of them.

  1. On each dial open the game menu and choose Connectivity.
  2. Set Room to the same number (1–9) on every dial. The room is joined when you tap Done, so scrolling past other rooms doesn't join them. Off unlinks the dial.
  3. Set This dial is player to the seat the dial belongs to. It changes straight away, and the dial opens on that player.
  4. The dots show the seats: filled where a dial sits, ringed for this dial, with the count of other dials underneath.
Who keeps their game? The first dial in a room keeps its game. Dials that join later take on the room's game. Use different room numbers for two tables near each other.

12Phone app

Run the whole table from a phone's bigger screen. A dial makes its own Wi-Fi network and serves the app, so there is nothing to install on iPhone or Android.

  1. On the dial: game menu → Connectivity → Phone → Turn on. The dial remembers this after a restart.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera to join the Wi-Fi. Network dueldial password dualdial
  3. The app usually opens by itself. If not, tap the QR code on the dial to switch to the app link and scan that, or open http://192.168.4.1.
  4. Optional: in Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen gives it an app icon.

13Home, reset and tips

Go home without losing the game

Press the screen three times quickly from anywhere (except while typing a name). The game selector opens with Resume highlighted, so one more press puts you straight back.

Reset for a new game

Press the screen in and keep holding. After about a second a red ring counts down; at 3 seconds the game selector opens so you can choose the next game. Let go before the ring fills to cancel.

Tips

  • Everything saves automatically and survives power-off.
  • Undo remembers 40 steps, from any dial or the phone.
  • The screen turns off after the sleep timer; the first touch or turn only wakes it.
  • A dial that loses power or wanders out of range catches up with the room's game within a couple of seconds of coming back.
